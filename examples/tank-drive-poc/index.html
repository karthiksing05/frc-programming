What this subsystem does

A tank drive has a left side and a right side, each driven by one or more motors. To turn, the two sides spin at different speeds. The classic mapping from a single joystick is called arcade drive:

   leftMotor  = forward + rotation
   rightMotor = forward - rotation

Push forward → both wheels spin forward. Push sideways → one side speeds up, the other slows down → robot rotates.

Your task

  1. Read through the starter DriveSubsystem.java on the right.
  2. Inside periodic(), fill in the // TODO lines so that:
    • forwardClean = your MathUtils.applyDeadband(forward, DEADBAND)
    • rotationClean = your MathUtils.applyDeadband(rotation, DEADBAND)
    • The arcade-drive equations above are applied
    • The values reach m_leftMotor.set(...) and m_rightMotor.set(...)
  3. Press Run — the robot on the right starts taking commands from your code.
  4. Drive with WASD (or the on-screen pad).
expand_more Hint 
double forwardClean  = MathUtils.applyDeadband(forward, DEADBAND);
double rotationClean = MathUtils.applyDeadband(rotation, DEADBAND);

double leftOut  = forwardClean + rotationClean;
double rightOut = forwardClean - rotationClean;

m_leftMotor.set(leftOut);
m_rightMotor.set(rightOut);

Goal check

  • Without pressing any keys, the robot should sit still (deadband working).
  • Press W → robot drives forward.
  • Press A or D alone → robot rotates in place.
  • Press W+D → robot drives forward while curving right.
